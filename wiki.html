Kael Voss

The 7-Thread Bearer • The Constellation Center

7-ThreadsConstellationIgnar

Overview

Kael Voss is the central figure of the Constellation, born in the Ashen Quarter of Ignar. He is the first 7-thread bearer recorded in three millennia. His journey from dock work to stabilizing the dimensional boundary redefined the relationship between threading and the physical world.

"You became ready by moving forward, by doing the next thing, by facing whatever the world required of you."

Early Life

Raised in the harsh environment of Ignar, Kael learned the "arithmetic of survival" early. He worked the docks, carrying kelp paste, which provided the foundational discipline required for his later mastery of threading.

Abilities

  • 7-Thread Awareness: Capable of maintaining simultaneous awareness of all seven core essences.
  • Harmonic Coupling: The ability to synchronize with other threaders to amplify healing protocols.
  • Void Sensitivity: Detects structural changes in the Primordial Prison.
