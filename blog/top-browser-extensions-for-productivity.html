Top AI Browser Extensions for Productivity in 2024

Artificial Intelligence is transforming how we work. Browser extensions are the easiest way to integrate AI into your daily workflow. Here are the top AI extensions you should be using.

1. ChatGPT Sidebar

This extension brings ChatGPT to every website you visit. You can summarize articles, write emails, and explain complex concepts without leaving your tab.

2. Grammarly

More than just a spell checker, Grammarly now uses AI to help you rewrite sentences for better tone and clarity, making your professional communication much more effective.

3. Merlin

Merlin is an all-in-one AI assistant that works on Google Search, LinkedIn, and Gmail. It can help you draft replies and summarize long YouTube videos instantly.

4. Otter.ai

Perfect for meetings, Otter.ai's browser extension can join your Zoom or Google Meet calls to transcribe and summarize discussions in real-time.

5. Jasper Everywhere

For content creators, Jasper brings its powerful AI writing capabilities directly into your CMS, email, and social media platforms.

Conclusion

Integrating these AI tools into your browser can significantly boost your productivity by automating repetitive tasks and enhancing your writing capabilities.
